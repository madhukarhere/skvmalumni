Your Impact

Where Every Rupee Goes

100% of contributions are pooled by the Alumni Association committee and spent on the school and its students. Broadly, funds are allocated across three areas:

📚
≈ 50%

Student Scholarships

Fee support and learning materials for meritorious students from families with financial hardship, so no SKVM student is left behind.

🏗️
≈ 30%

School Infrastructure

Classroom repairs, library books, science-lab equipment, sports gear and other one-time capital needs the school requests each year.

🎉
≈ 20%

Events & Teacher Welfare

Annual alumni meet, teacher felicitations, cultural events and small welfare gestures for the staff who shaped us.

Percentages are indicative and adjusted each year by the committee based on the school's current needs.
